a note from levi

I trained as a chemical engineer, so I design brands the way engineers build plants: as systems. The logo is one part. The real work is making it hold up on a billboard, a uniform, an app screen and a stage, everywhere it shows up. Since 2022 I have done that for brands in nine countries. If it is going out into the world, I want it impossible to ignore.
