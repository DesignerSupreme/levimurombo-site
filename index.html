06 / About
About me

    

      

        

          
I started making movie posters as a teenager on my brother's laptop. Then I studied chemical and process systems engineering at Harare Institute of Technology and published research in Materials Today.

          
Design kept pulling me back. Since 2022 I've designed for brands in nine countries, and today I lead digital media and advertising at AURA Technologies.

          
On the side I build my own things: a watch brand, a 3D digital twin of Harare, and more posters than I can count.
